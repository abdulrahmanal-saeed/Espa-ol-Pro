# Curriculum

## Principles

- **Objectives first.** Each level lists CEFR-style "can-do" outcomes; units
  and lessons exist to serve them. Aligned to the CEFR Companion Volume and the
  thematic scope of the Instituto Cervantes Plan Curricular. No textbook,
  exam or third-party lesson text is copied: all content is original.
- **Sentence-first vocabulary.** Every item carries at least one example
  sentence with translation, and is practised in recognition, listening and
  production.
- **Retrieval over recognition.** Every lesson must contain production
  (typing, ordering, dictation, situational reply) and listening; multiple
  choice is used for first contact, not as the main proof of learning.
- **Use what the learner already knows.** Arabic and English connections are
  first-class content (`connections`): Arabic-origin words, cognates, false
  friends, pronunciation transfer (j ≈ خ, tapped r = ر, Castilian z ≈ ث, the
  e/i and o/u contrasts Arabic lacks), grammar parallels (gender agreement,
  usted ≈ حضرتك, profession without article).
- **Work from day one.** Professional vocabulary is tagged `work` and
  introduced from Pre-A1 (oficina, cliente, cita), growing into the
  "Spanish for Work" track.
- **Arabic etymologies.** The [RAE/ASALE entry for *hasta*](https://dle.rae.es/hasta)
  traces it through Andalusi Arabic to classical Arabic حتّى, with Latin
  influence. The [entry for *cero*](https://dle.rae.es/cero) traces it back
  through Italian, Late Latin and Andalusi Arabic to classical Arabic صِفْر.

A1 is complete in content: 23 lessons over 6 units, closed by six unit
checkpoints and the A1 level assessment `lvl_a1`, which opens only when every
A1 lesson is completed. A present-tense **verb reference** (16 verbs, all six
persons, regular forms machine-checked) is in Library → الأفعال.

A2 begins with A2-U1 (lessons 33–37, content 1.5.0): banks, post, repairs and
complaints, ending with the learner on the provider's side of a complaint.
Its first lesson follows the last A1 lesson. The A1 level assessment stays
visible on the path until it has been taken once.

A2-U2 (lessons 38–42, content 1.6.0) teaches the pretérito indefinido in
five steps: regular -ar, then -er/-ir with sequencing, then the yo spelling
changes, then the irregular ir/ser, hacer and tener, and finally a connected
client report contrasting past events with the present state. Lesson 41 is
the first place irregular past forms are practised or assessed. The verb
reference now shows the past of all 32 verbs.

A2-U3 (lessons 43–47, content 1.7.0) moves from intentions (ir a) to
invitations, arranging and confirming meetings, timetables in the present, and
a project plan with si + present.

A2-U4 (lessons 48–52, content 1.8.0) covers likes (gustar), choosing
(preferir), comparison, polite opinions, and a recommendation between two
supplier offers.

A2-U5 (lessons 53–57, content 1.9.0) closes A2 with written and spoken work
communication: formal emails, quick messages in the right register, a new
client enquiry, a follow-up that combines past, present and future, and
closing a meeting. All five A2 units are now available.

The A2 level assessment `lvl_a2` (content 1.10.0) closes A2 in the same
format as `lvl_a1`. It has 25 questions over five can-dos, one per unit,
and covers five skills with no speaking. Five questions play a sentence
from each unit's dialogues. It opens only when every A2 lesson is
completed, and its tile sits at the end of A2 on the path. Like every
checkpoint here, it is practice evidence, not a CEFR certificate.

B1 requires passing `lvl_a2` (owner decision, PHASE4-001). An attempt
passes when every can-do in it is met at the can-do threshold (75 %). Any
passing attempt opens B1 for good; a later failed retry does not close it.
A1 → A2 still needs no level-test pass. The rule is `requires_pass` in
`curriculum.json` and is derived from stored attempts, so it adds no learner
state and survives backup and restore.

B1-U1 (lessons 58–62, content 1.11.0) teaches opinion with reasons,
connectors, the simple conditional (regular, then irregular) for wishes,
polite requests and advice, and a structured argument in a meeting. It
avoids structures that need the subjunctive (no creo que…, para que…,
sería mejor que…) until B1-U3, and the imperfect until B1-U2. The verb
reference adds the conditional of 11 verbs and three new verbs (deber,
importar, convencer).

Owner decisions after B1-U1 (content 1.12.0):

- **Accents count from B1.** From B1 on, a missing written accent in a typed
  answer is a mistake, not a reminder. A1 and A2 are unchanged.
- **Spanish-first instructions from B1-U2.** Selected comprehension
  instructions are in Spanish, with the Arabic one a tap away. More follow in
  later units. Grammar explanations stay in Arabic.
- **B2 will require passing `lvl_b1`**, as B1 requires `lvl_a2`. The gate is
  added together with `lvl_b1`, because the validator needs the assessment
  to exist.
- **Longer reading and listening in B1.** From B1-U2 each unit adds an
  extended reading text and a longer listening. B1-U1 gets them
  retroactively in a follow-up PR.

B1-U2 (lessons 63–68, content 1.13.0) teaches the imperfect: habits, then
description with ser / ir / ver, then the contrast with the indefinido. It
applies that to a professional story and to a project incident. Lesson 68 is
the first longer reading and listening lesson: one ~150-word text with four
questions, and a 12-turn dialogue. It is also the first content with
Spanish-first instructions. The verb reference now shows the imperfect of
every verb.

Totals (content 1.13.0, from the validator): 68 lessons, 574 items,
1930 activities (1212 graded), 76 grammar notes, 38 dialogues, 18 assessments,
61 verbs.

## Lesson shape

`note` (concept) → `teach` (item, audio, examples, connections) → recognition
→ listening → guided production (fill, order) → free production (type,
dictation) → situational reply or reading → listen-and-repeat → summary →
FSRS cards. The validator enforces: every item taught before practised and
practised at least once in a graded activity; ≥ 6 graded activities of ≥ 4
types; at least one production and one listening activity.
